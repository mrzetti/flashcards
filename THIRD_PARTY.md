## Ruffle

The self-hosted runtime in `assets/ruffle/` is Ruffle 0.6.0, copied byte-for-byte
from the existing Benzin preservation project's vendored distribution.
Upstream: https://ruffle.rs/ and https://github.com/ruffle-rs/ruffle.
Distribution: https://www.npmjs.com/package/@ruffle-rs/ruffle/v/0.6.0.
The package metadata and Apache-2.0 / MIT license texts accompany the runtime.
