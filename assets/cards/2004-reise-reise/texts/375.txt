FOR RAMMSTEIN. 'Reise, Reise' - the Berlin band's fourth studio outing since 1995 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
- is a collection of 'firsts': the first time the six have recorded in Spain (El Cortijo 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
Studio, Malaga); the first time bassist Oli has sung on an album (he underpins Till's 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
more dramatic vocal moments); the first time Schneider has torn up the rhythm 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
rulebook (check out 'Keine Lust'/'No Desire' for Rammstein 'shuffle' nummer 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
eins!), the first time the group have worked on songs they weren't all committed 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
to right from the start (some of which are now amongst their favourites); the 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
first time they've recorded a track as in-your-face commercial as second single/ 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
video 'Amerika' or as stripped-to-the-groove acoustic as 'Los'/ 'Let's Go', and - 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
as mentioned above - the first time in a long time the recording vibe could 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
genuinely be called relaxed. 'Fun' even.
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
So does 'Reise, Reise' still sound like Rammstein? 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
Absolutely! 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
Many of the band's signature motifs continue to strike a major chord - the extra 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
widescreen production (courtesy of Jacob Hellner, who has been integral to the 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
studio side since day one), the German vocals (with the exception of 'Amerika', 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
which switches in and out of English, and  'Moskau', where Till gets some 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
distinctive Russian back-up) and the ability to write songs that, in a just & fair 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
society, would be adopted as anthems by independent states the world over. 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
However, from the band's point of view, 'Reise, Reise' (either 'Journey, Journey' 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
or 'Voyage, Voyage', depending how literary you wish you go!) represents a move 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
that takes them well beyond the comfort zone - voyage, voyage, indeed...
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
"For me, this project marks a new beginning," says Schneider. "After the 'Mutter' 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
album (2001), we found it difficult to be with each other, because our egos were 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
causing a problem. There was a lot of tension in the way we were working 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
together, and I guess we were using that competitive atmosphere, the aggression, 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
to drive the creative process...
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
"Certainly, we achieved good results that way, but for this new album we decided 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
to do things differently; we spent a couple of months apart, and that made us 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
realise just how much we'd accomplished over the years. So when we went into 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
the studio this time, we made sure that the atmosphere was a lot lighter, and that 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
we explored each other's ideas in full. If we hadn't done that, we would never 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
have emerged with a song such as 'Los'..."
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
NOW CONFIRMED for release on September 27 2004, 'Reise, Reise' - 11 tracks, 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
nigh on 50 minutes of music - is without doubt Rammstein's most complete set of 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
recordings, taking the band into areas that - under the old working regime - could 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
easily have remained unexplored. Would 'Mutter'-era Rammstein have been quite 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
so keen to turn off the distortion ('Los') or dismiss their preferred military two-
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
step in favour of a full-tilt stomp ('Keine Lust')? Probably not. But now, after an 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
extended (though fruitful) period in a black & white world, Rammstein 2004 are 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
starting to see the grey, and having a damn good time doing it...
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
Says Richard: "With 'Mutter', there was very little flexibility involved, it was almost 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
like we were working towards some sort of a 'masterplan'; plus, that was a very 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
hard record to make from an emotional point of view, because as six strong 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
individuals we were all trying to make ourselves heard...
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
"This time, however, we've done things on the recording side that we wouldn't 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
have in the past, including writing in the studio, something that gave us a real 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
sense of freedom. But I honestly don't think we could have reached the place we 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
are now without going through the whole 'Mutter' experience - we survived that, 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
and it made us all a lot stronger..."
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
Certainly, 'Reise, Reise' (the album) shows a band prepared to ditch the 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
'masterplan' in favour of an approach that, by previous standards, could almost 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
be described as 'reckless'. Since the release of their 'Herzeleid' debut back in 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
1995, the Rammstein reputation has been hammered out on words such as 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
'disciplined' & 'precise'; here was an act whose power seemed to stem from their 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
symmetry, their ability to work in unison, to deliver the well-aimed (musical) blow… 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
then deliver it again. Naturally, that quality continues to have its place, but 'Reise, 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
Reise' - tracked over a six-week period & mixed in Stockholm by Stefan 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
Glaumann, reprising his role on 'Mutter' - is a 'rock'n'roll' album at heart, packed 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
with raw power ('Stein Um Stein', etc.) and raw emotion ('Ohne Dich', 'Amour', 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
etc.) in equal part;  a record that moves like a well-oiled machine, but with a 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
human hand very much at the controls...
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
From drums through to vocals and all points inbetween, Rammstein have created 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
a body of work that not only frees up their collective skills, but moves those skills 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
to a higher, more spontaneous level, the whole thing supported by mandolins & 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
accordions, choir & orchestra parts. Whether or not Till actually went to train his 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
vocals in a local monastery, as a poker-faced Flake will tell you if you ask, must 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
remain the subject of some debate, but there's no denying that - with or without 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
monkish aid - these are his finest vocal performances to date.
