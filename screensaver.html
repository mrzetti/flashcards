This page is not the original program. It rebuilds the screensaver’s look from its own extracted bitmaps: the screen frame, the 21 lyric plates, the RAMMSTEIN wordmark and the animation frames of the crosses and flames. The original runs only on 32-bit Windows, and it exits as soon as the pointer moves; the downloads on the card include it alongside the complete extraction.
